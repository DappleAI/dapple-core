This policy explains what you can do with the marks without asking, and when you need permission. Its goal is simple: people should be able to tell whether something comes from DappleAI Co. or not.

## The marks

- The names **Dapple**, **DappleAI** and **Dapple AI**, and the product name **Dapple Prompt**
- The Dapple logos, including the files in `crates/dapple-render/assets/images/` and any other Dapple logo in this repository
- The "Contextualized by Dapple AI" colophon and wordmark that the render pipeline adds to generated documents

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** For example: "built on Dapple Prompt", "a fork of Dapple Prompt", "compatible with the Dapple API", or "we contributed to DappleAI's open-source code".
- **Use and redistribute the unmodified code** under its original name, keeping the copyright, license, NOTICE and trademark files intact.
- **Write about the project.** Blog posts, tutorials, talks, reviews, news articles and academic work can use the names, and can show the logo to illustrate the project, as long as they don't suggest DappleAI Co. wrote, sponsored or endorsed them.
- **Keep the generated colophon.** Documents rendered by unmodified code carry the "Contextualized by Dapple AI" colophon; leaving it in place is fine.
- **Name your fork's origin.** A modified version can say it's "based on Dapple Prompt" in its README and documentation.

## What needs written permission

- **Naming a fork, product, service or company with the marks.** A modified version must ship under a different name and logo, one that isn't confusingly similar (for example, not "Dapple Plus" or "DappleAI Cloud").
- **Offering a hosted service under the marks.** You can host the software, but don't call the service Dapple or present it as run by DappleAI Co.
- **Using a mark in a domain name, app name, package name or social media handle** for something that isn't run by DappleAI Co.
- **Using the logos** in a way not listed above, including modified versions of them, or on merchandise.
- **Implying endorsement, partnership or certification** by DappleAI Co.

### Rendered documents from modified code

The render pipeline (`crates/dapple-render`) stamps a "Contextualized by Dapple AI" colophon and wordmark on every document. If you modify the software, especially the content pipeline, remove or replace that colophon. Otherwise documents you produce would carry our name on output we didn't make.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue in this repository titled **"Trademark permission request"** that describes what you'd like to do, or ask a maintainer. Don't include anything confidential in a public issue; a maintainer will follow up privately if needed.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the marks used in a way that confuses people about what's official, open an issue or use GitHub's **Report content** feature.
