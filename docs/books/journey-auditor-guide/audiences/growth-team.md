# Growth Team

Marketing and Sales stakeholders focused on journey visibility, conversion metrics, and revenue attribution.

---

## Role Definition

<!-- ANCHOR: audience-growth-team -->
The Growth Team encompasses Marketing and Sales functions that need visibility into:
- How users discover and select Playbooks
- Journey completion rates and abandonment points
- Revenue attribution across campaigns and channels
- Conversion funnel metrics from trial to paid

### Key Decisions
- Campaign effectiveness and budget allocation
- Product positioning and messaging
- Sales qualification criteria
- Pricing and packaging feedback
<!-- /ANCHOR -->

---

## Audit Questions

Questions the Growth Team typically needs answered:

### Journey Visibility
- Which Playbooks have the highest adoption rates?
- Where do users abandon Journeys?
- What's the average time from Journey start to completion?
- How do completion rates vary by user segment?

### Conversion Metrics
- What's the trial-to-paid conversion rate by Playbook?
- Which Playbooks drive the most upgrades?
- How long is the typical sales cycle?
- What touchpoints correlate with conversion?

### Revenue Attribution
- Which campaigns drive the highest LTV customers?
- What's the revenue contribution by channel?
- How does Playbook usage correlate with expansion revenue?
- Which user actions predict churn vs. renewal?

---

## Self-Service vs. Escalation

### Self-Service (via dashboards/reports)
- Journey completion rates
- Playbook adoption metrics
- Basic conversion funnels
- Campaign attribution (with proper UTM tracking)

### Escalation to Engineering/Data
- Custom cohort analysis
- Complex attribution models
- Data quality issues in tracking
- New metric definitions
